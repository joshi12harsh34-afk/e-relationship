## GENERAL

The scope of this supply is to provide one package (22-PK-3901) to homogenize PP pellets in 8 Blender Silos (not in the scope of the supply) or to transfer PP pellets from one Blender to another one (transferring ISBL) and to convey pellets to Bagging unit.

## 1. DESCRIPTION OF PNEUMATIC HOMOGENISATION/TRANSFERRING ISBL (LINE A) (according to P&IDs 1916-22-390,391A,391B,391C,391D,391E,391F,391G,391H)

Pneumatic homogenization takes place in an open dilute phase circulation system.

The homogenization process requires a filled Pellets Blender Silo (see 22-PK-3804) with analysed pellets as a pre-condition.

As only one homogenization operation of a Pellets Blender Silo 22-V-3901A-H at the same time is possible, it is necessary to choose one of the eight Pellets Blender Silos which is the first or the next to be homogenised.

The homogenization takes place by circulating the pellets pneumatically from the silo bottom to the top. For the purpose of blending, the pellets are drawn off from the outlet cone of the Blender Silo as well as from different Silo levels, enabled by several mixing pipes with intake openings at different Silo levels.

The pellets circulation system receives the polymer by gravity from the mixing part of the Silo (ambient operating pressure) via a Rotary Feeder (22-ME-3901A-H) and a Diverter Valve which is switched to position “L”. Via a Line Injector (Product Intake Shoe), the pellets are sent to the top of the Blender Silo by means of conveying air.

The conveying air is generated by a Pneumatic Transport Blower station consisting of three Blower units (22-K-3901A-C). Via Line A (to 22-ME-3901A-H) the Blower 22-K-3901A - together with its Air Intake Filter 22-G-3901A and its Discharge Cooler 22-E-3901A serves for homogenization/transferring ISBL purposes only, whereas the Transport Blower 22-K-3901C serves as a common spare for both purposes, i.e. homogenization/transferring ISBL and pellet transport to the Bagging (see Chapter 2).

The conveying pellets recycle design capacity shall be 70000 kg/hr, whereas the homogenization period shall be calculated as one complete circulation of the Silo content.

The Vendor shall offer his best technology for pellet transport and shall offer improvements if required.

## 2. DESCRIPTION OF PNEUMATIC TRANSPORT TO BAGGING OSBL (LINE B)

(according to P&IDs 1916-22-390,391A,391B,391C,391D,391E,391F,391G,391H)

The pneumatic transport takes place in an open dilute phase transfer system.

The Transfer Systems (22-PK-3901) receive the polymer by gravity from the mixing part of the Pellets Blender Silos 22-V-3901A-H, which are operated at ambient pressure. Via Rotary Feeders 22-ME-3901A-H, a Diverter Valve which is switched to position “H” and a Line Injector (Product Intake Shoe) the pellets are pneumatically conveyed to the Bagging system (out of the scope of supply). The transport of the pellets to the Bagging system is possible only from one Pellets Blender Silo at the same time.

The conveying air will be generated by a Pneumatic Transport Blower station consisting of three Blower Units (22-K-3901A-C). Via line B (to 22-ME-3901A-H) the Blower 22-K-3901B - together with their Air Intake Filters 22-G-3901B and Discharge Coolers 22-E-3901B - serves for the pellets transport to the Bagging system only. The Blower station 22-K-3901C (including 22-G-3901C, 22-E-3901C) serves as a common spare for both purposes, i.e. homogenization / transferring ISBL and pellet transport to the Bagging (see Chapter 1).

The pellets conveying design capacity to the Bagging shall be 70000 kg/hr.

The Vendor shall offer his best technology for the pellets transport and shall offer improvements if required.

## 3. DESCRIPTION OF SILO VENTING BLOWER

(according to PIDs 1916-22-390,391A,391B,391C,391D,391E,391F,391G,391H)

The Silo Venting System (not in the scope of 22-PK-3901 Vendor) consists of two Silo Venting Blowers (dedicated to all silos and acting each other as back-up) including Intake Filters and a Cooler (if necessary).

For the purpose of stripping Peroxide decomposition products from the pellets in Silos 22-V-3901A-H, the air delivery capacity (as volumetric flow) at Blower discharge conditions is calculated as $20\mathrm{m}^3/\mathrm{hr}$ for each t/hr of polymer produced per each Silo ventilated. For this project it is $8400\mathrm{m}^3/\mathrm{hr}$.

Venting of Silos will take place whenever a Silo contains Visbroken products, during all stages (loading-homogenization-unloading-temporary storage).

\- 22-LALL-39135 (level low in 22-V-3901A) is active.

The pneumatic haulage STOP sequence takes place in reverse order.
