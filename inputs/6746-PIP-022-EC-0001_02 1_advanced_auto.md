## 1 General

This technical specification describes the scope of supply and technical requirements for delivery of Package Unit “Pneumatic Conveying Package” for a IOCL, PANIPAT REFINERY”.

The intent of this specification is to outline the purchaser's requirements for design, engineering, procurement of raw materials and bought outs components, manufacturing, assembly, inspection, testing, painting, supply supervision of installation and commissioning of all Pneumatic conveying systems.

All equipment and materials supplied under these specifications shall be designed and manufactured in accordance with attached specifications, drawings and other documents. No omission in these specifications shall relieve the contractor of his responsibilities.

The system supplied shall be complete in every respect ready for operation and any item not specifically mentioned in this specification but nevertheless required for proper operation, maintenance and safety of the equipment / personnel shall be deemed to have been specified by the purchaser and shall be included in the scope of supply of the bidder.

Bidder to propose and submit the complete schedule for engineering & manufacturing, Supervision of Erection & site work, Testing & Commissioning.

## 6 Scope Of Supply

Bidder's scope of supply for pneumatic conveying systems includes below four different conveying systems.

22-PK-3801 : Polymer Pneumatic Transport to Intermediate Surge Silos.

22-PK-3804 : PP pellets pneumatic transport from Extruder to Homogenizing Silos.

22-PK-3901 : Homogenization of PP Pellets in the pellets blender silos 22-V-3901A- H and transport of pellets to the bagging unit.

22-PK-3806: Talc Transport system.

22-PK-3904 Blenders Blowing system

Note – Refer licenser's data sheet for system description of all above four systems.

The scope of this specification covers the Design, Detail engineering, Fabrication, Supply of materials, Inspection, Testing, Packing, loading, Transportation to site, Supervision of Erection & Commissioning, Trial run, and Guarantee to meet the requirement set forth in this specification indicated above and data sheet.
