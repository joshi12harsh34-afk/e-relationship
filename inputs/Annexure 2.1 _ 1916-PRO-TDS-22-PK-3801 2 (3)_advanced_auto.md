## 1. PNEUMATIC HAULAGE OPERATION DESCRIPTION (for reference see PI&D No. 1916-22-380A, 1916-22-353)

Pneumatic haulage consists of a closed loop transfer line. The system receives the polymer from the Dryer (operating pressure 0.1 kg/cm²(g) through Feeding Hopper 22-V-3800) From 22-V-3800 a Rotary Feeder 22-ME-3801A (or 22-ME-3801B) sends it to the Intermediate Surge Silo 22-V-3801A/B (1000 m³ of capacity, with 90% filling).

The transport is achieved by means of two blowers, 22-K-3801A&B. 22-K-3801B acts as spare for A. Nitrogen from 22-V-3801A/B top, after filtration through the Guard Filters 22-G-3801A/B, is recovered at the suction of 22-K-3801A/B.

Nitrogen vents from the rotary feeders, after filtration through the filter 22-G-3800 (self-cleaning bag type) installed on the Hopper 22-V-3800, are recovered at the suction of the blowers 22-K-3801A/B.

The Coolers (22-E-3800,22-E-3801), located 22-E-3800 on suction and 22-E-3801 on delivery of blowers, are envisaged in order to avoid the heating of the product and to take out the blower compression heat (no product temperature increase according to a product feeding temperature of 90°C max.).

In order to avoid air infiltration, the pressure in the closed circuit must be maintained everywhere above the atmosphere.

Supplier has to design the circuit in order to guarantee an operating pressure in the Intermediate Surge Silo 22-V-3801A/B (Design Pressure of silo: -0.005/+0.15 kg/cm²(g) as low as possible (but in any case above the atmosphere) in order to permit the nitrogen return line to be recycled to blowers suction 22-K-3801A/B without (if possible) any additional booster fan.

Silo 22-V-3801A/B is installed on weight cells for weight control.

A minor feed (2000 kg/hr max.), consisting of PP powder fines (particle size <105  $\mu$ m), is fed directly to the pneumatic haulage pipe downstream to the two Rotary Feeders 22-ME-3801A/B. This stream comes from the Dryer Cyclone 22-S-3502 (not included in the supply) and it's continuously discharged to the Fines Discharge Hopper 22-V-3503 (included in the scope of supply) by means of the Rotary Feeder 22-ME-3803A (included in the scope of supply). PP powder is then fed to the pneumatic transport by means of the Rotary Feeder 22-ME-3803B (included in the scope of supply). The Hopper shall be provided with a self-cleaning bag type Filter 22-G-3508 (included in the supply), which is furnished with timer acting solenoid valves on the nitrogen cleaning supply. Line injector, that discharges fines into the main transport line, shall be designed in order to avoid troubles to the main flow in the pneumatic haulage pipe coming from Rotary Feeders 22-ME-3801A/B.

## 7.1. Pneumatic Haulage start-up sequence

This sequence has to be applied prior to initial start-up of closed loop system. To remove the existing oxygen to a concentration below 4% the whole system has to be purged with nitrogen.

The initial purging of the closed loop system will be performed in 2 steps:

- Step 1: filling of conveying system and silo 22-V-3801A/B with nitrogen, without running Blowers 22-K-3801A or B.
- Step 2: starting the selected Blowers and run the system without product, while feeding nitrogen from the nitrogen feed-in unit till the required oxygen concentration (< 4%) will be measured by the gas analyser (22-Al-38001). This is to be understood to evacuate air from the close loop system by using the nitrogen feeding.

1) Align 22-G-3801A (or B) to the pneumatic haulage.
2) Activation of the Silo Top Filters 22-G-3820A/B (start filter cleaning by nitrogen flushing).
3) Open nitrogen make-up.
4) Open 22-XV-38005 or 22-XV-38015 (22-K-3801A and 22-K-3801B by-pass) according to the selected blower (22-HS-38005 Or 22-HS-38015 respectively).
5) If 22-K-3801A has been selected:
- Open 22-HV-38004 by means of 22-HS-38004.
If 22-K-3801B has been selected:
- Open 22-HV-38014 by means of 22-HS-38014.
6) Start the selected blower (22-K-3801A or B) by means of 22-HS-38002B START push button and after a certain time set by a timer, open delivery valve 22-XV-38006 (or 22-XV-38007), while by-pass valve (22-XV-38005 OR 22-XV-38015) begins to close slowly. Blower can start only after verification of the right position of the valves involved in the selected path (consent from their limit switches).
7) Total filling of the circuit with nitrogen, introduced under control of 22-PIC-38101 and of oxygen analyzers 22-Al-38001.
8) Start the rotary valves 22-ME-3801A or 22-ME-3801B by means of 22-HS-38010 and verify the correct position of 22-XV-38001A and 22-XV-38001B (ESD 3804).
10) When oxygen is below the set point of analyzers 22-Al-38001, it is possible to start the rotary valve 22-ME-3801A or B, by means of 22-HS-38001A push button. The running of the rotary feeder 22-ME-3800 is a condition necessary to open the valve 22-LV-35301 (ESD 3801).
