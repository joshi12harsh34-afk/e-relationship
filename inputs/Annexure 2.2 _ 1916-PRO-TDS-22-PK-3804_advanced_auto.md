## 1 PNEUMATIC HAULAGE OPERATION DESCRIPTION

(for reference see PIDs No. 1916-22-31813)

Transport consists of one open circuit transfer line with one Pneumatic Haulage Station equipped with 2 Blowers 22-K-3804A/B (one as stand-by).

PP pellets, coming from the Extrusion unit, are continuously transferred by means of Pneumatic Transport Package 22-PK-3804 from Feeding Hopper 22-V-3807 to Homogenizing Silos.

Pellets flowrate fed to the Pneumatic Transport can increase/decrease continuously from minimum to the design flowrate; Vendor shall take into consideration this, to properly design the system. Vendor shall guarantee proper functioning of blower for the entire operating range of plant

Pneumatic Transport Cooler 22-E-3804 is provided in order to remove the Blower compression heat.

A suitable interlocks system provides all the interlocks and consents for a safe and easy remote operation of the conveyor system.

## 4 PIPING REQUIREMENTS

- Straight pipes shall be 304 Stainless Steel or Hardened Aluminium;
- Elbows shall be 304 Stainless Steel.

Clean out flow connections, if required, must be installed at strategic locations along the pipelines in order to eliminate eventual product plugs.

Piping joints shall be flanged.

## 7.1.1. PNEUMATIC HAULAGE START-UP SEQUENCE (ESD 3901)

1) Select one of the Eight Pellet Blender Silos 22-V-3901A-H to which the pellets shall be conveyed.
2) By means of the relevant push button 22-HS-39130 / 39140 / 39150 / 39160 / 39170 / 39180 / 39190 / 39103 the selected blender silo will be lined up automatically by switching the involved diverter valves (verification to ESD-3901 by reaching the relevant ZSL-end positions). A condition necessary for this purpose: the selected Silo is not full, i.e. 22-LAHH-39131 / 39141 / 39151 / 39161 / 39171 / 39181 / 39191 / 39101 is not active.
3) Open 22-XV-38908 (22-ZSL-38903 is open) or 22-XV-38904 (22-ZSL-38904 is open) according to the selected blower 22-K-3804 A or B. The correct OPEN/CLOSE positions of 22-ZSL-38903 / 22-ZSL-38904 are a condition necessary to start the selected blower
4) Blower start-up (22-K-3804A or B) by means of 22-HS-38911 or 22-HS-38912 START push button. The Blower can start only after verification of the right position of all diverter valves, involved in the selected path.
5) Select the Rotary Valve 22-ME-3805A or B, which will feed the Pneumatic Haulage by means of 22-HS-38914 push button, verify the aligned position of Slide Gate Valve 22-XV-38905 A or B towards the selected Rotary Feeder, then check the correct position of the Slide Gate Valves 22-XV-38904 A or B to be aligned via their limit switches.
6) Now, by means of 22-HS-38915 START the operator start feeding material into the system. Start the pre-selected Rotary Feeder 22-ME-3805A or B.
Now the pellets transport to the selected Blender Silo is in operation.

The Pneumatic Haulage STOP-sequence takes place in reverse order. 22-HS-38913 is provided to deactivate ESD 3901

Remark: The Vendor shall offer and explain a complete automatic START/STOP-sequence control, which will be approved by LYONDELLBASELL
